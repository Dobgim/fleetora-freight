Common questions

Our team replies within the hour on business days. All questions

How fast will I get a quote?

Most quotes go out within 2 business hours. Oversized, multi stop or dangerous goods shipments can take up to 24 hours because we confirm space with the carrier first.

Where do I find my tracking number?

It is in your booking confirmation email. It starts with FLE followed by three groups of four letters and numbers, for example FLE-7KQ4-M29X-48HD. Enter it on the tracking page.

Do you handle customs clearance?

Yes. Our licensed brokers file the import and export paperwork, classify your goods and pay duties on your behalf so the shipment is not held.

Is my cargo insured?

Carriers only pay a small amount per kilogram if something goes wrong. Our cargo insurance covers the full value of your goods for loss and damage. Add it when you request a quote.
